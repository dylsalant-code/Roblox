# Visual Identity

## Name
**Catch a Cloudling**. It follows the "Verb a Noun" pattern players already search for ("Grow a Garden", "Steal a Brainrot"), and the alliteration makes it easy to say. "Cloudling" is ownable, cute and searchable.

Search keywords for the description: *catch, cute pets, clouds, rainbow, tycoon, simulator, collect, idle, weather, sky island*.

## Palette
| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Sky / base | Pastel lavender | `#EBE4FF` |
| Primary accent | Sparkle gold | `#FFCD46` |
| Brand rainbow | Coral → Orange → Yellow → Mint → Sky → Violet → Orchid | `#FF5A6E #FFA046 #FFE150 #6EDC78 #5AB4FF #8C78FF #DC78FF` |
| UI text / outlines | Deep plum | `#462D6E` |
| Success / Shop / Rebirth | Mint `#5AD278`, Gold `#FFCD46`, Violet `#966EFF` |

Font: **Fredoka One** for everything game-facing. It's round, friendly and readable on small phones. UI elements use thick plum outlines and white-to-tint gradients for a "sticker" look.

## Logo concept
- The word **CLOUDLING** in chunky white Fredoka letters with a thick plum outline. A small rainbow arcs over the "OU", and the dot of the "i" is a tiny gold ✨.
- "Catch a" sits above in smaller gold script-style letters.
- A Puffle (white round Cloudling with pink cheeks) peeks out from behind the "G".

## Icon concept (512×512)
- Big, centered **Puffle face**: white sphere, glossy black oval eyes with white shine, pink cheeks, tiny smile.
- It sits on a fluffy cloud, with a **rainbow arc** behind it on a bright sky-blue gradient.
- One gold ✨ in the top-right corner.
- No text, because icons are tiny on mobile. The face reads at 50×50 px.

## Thumbnail concepts (1920×1080)
1. **The Race**: 4 avatars sprinting along the rainbow stream toward a glowing **SECRET Starwhale** with a "?!" bubble. Big text: "CATCH THE SECRET!"

Rotate the thumbnails monthly, A/B test them in Creator Dashboard, and keep the one with the highest click-through rate.

## In-game look (already implemented)
- Floating grass islands with sandy undersides and cloud puffs, connected by white bridges to a lavender promenade.
- The **Rainbow Stream** is 7 neon stripes, under a **giant neon rainbow arch** with the game title. This is the screenshot moment.
- The Cloud Gate and Sky Drain arches mark where the stream starts and ends. Cloud clusters drift in all directions.
- Lighting: Atmosphere haze, bloom, light saturation boost and sun rays. Weather retints the world and changes the time of day.
- Cloudlings: round bodies with faces and per-species features (ears, horns, wings, crowns, halos, antlers...). Epic+ glow, mutated ones sparkle, Rainbow ones cycle colors.
